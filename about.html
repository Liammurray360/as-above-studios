About the studio

Who we are

At As Above Audio Studio, we pride ourselves on guiding you through the recording process seamlessly and effortlessly.

We aim to create a laid-back environment for musicians of all genres and strive to make your sonic vision come to life.

Operating in Northwold near Northgate, we offer a full recording studio experience from live tracking to complete productions from start to finish.

We also provide live sound rentals with engineers to make your event sound and look exceptional.

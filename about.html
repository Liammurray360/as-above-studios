WHO ARE WE?

At As Above Audio Studio, we pride ourselves on guiding you through the recording process seamlessly and effortlessly.

We aim to create a laid-back environment for any kind of musician and strive to make your sonic vision come to life.

Operating in Northwold near Northgate, we offer a full recording studio experience, from live tracks to full studio songs from start to finish, and much more.

In addition, we also offer live sound rentals with engineers to make your show sound and look good.
